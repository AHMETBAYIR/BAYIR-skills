## Copyright

TS 500, TBDY 2018, TS EN and AASHTO are copyrighted. Do not reproduce full
tables. Use a small, cited subset; write clause numbers rather than copying
pages.

## What not to cite

- A forum post as a code.
- An undated PDF as an edition.
- A manufacturer's span table as a design code.
- A screenshot of ETABS/SAP2000 as verification.
- An AI-generated “code clause”.

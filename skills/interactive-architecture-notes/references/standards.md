current edition and the national annex before quoting a clause.

## Copyright

Turkish standards (TS, TS EN) and TBDY are copyrighted. Do not reproduce a
full table or clause in a note. Use a small subset with a citation, and write
the clause number rather than copying the page.

## What not to cite

- A manufacturer's brochure as a code.
- A blog as a standard.
- An undated PDF as an edition.
- A render as a measurement.
